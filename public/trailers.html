Submission Protocol

To maintain our standard, we do not accept loose YouTube links. Strictly follow the steps below:

01

Download the Template

Download our official PSD file. This is the mandatory base template for your Thumbnail, which must be submitted alongside your trailer.

Download Official PSD ↓
02

Prepare the Assets

Render the video in at least 1080p (60fps). Save your final Thumbnail in high quality based on our official template.

03

Compress into a ZIP

Place strictly the video and the final Thumbnail image into a single .ZIP file. The final file must not exceed 2GB.

Submit Your Project
